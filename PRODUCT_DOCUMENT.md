# RecoverAI Product Document

## 1. Problem Statement
Cart abandonment is the biggest revenue leak in e-commerce, costing businesses billions annually. Currently, most checkout recovery relies on static, rule-based email sequences (e.g., sending the same 10% discount email 24 hours later). This approach ignores the nuanced reasons *why* a user abandoned their cart (e.g., price friction vs. shipping costs vs. technical errors) and relies on margin-destroying blanket discounts. 

## 2. The Solution: RecoverAI
RecoverAI is a dynamic, context-aware AI recovery engine. Instead of static follow-ups, RecoverAI intercepts abandoned checkout signals and passes the context (cart value, customer tier, product type, friction point) into a Large Language Model (Groq LLaMA-3). 

The AI dynamically selects the optimal psychological strategy (e.g., Scarcity, Social Proof, Reminder, or Discount) to recover the cart while protecting profit margins, generating highly personalized, persuasive copy on the fly.

## 3. Core Features
- **Dynamic Strategy Selection:** AI assesses cart value and customer tier to pick the highest-converting strategy without defaulting to discounts.
- **Margin Protection Logic:** High-value VIPs might receive discounts, but low-value carts receive scarcity or social proof triggers, protecting the bottom line.
- **Real-Time Simulation Console:** A sleek, dark-mode cyber-intelligence dashboard that visualizes the AI's reasoning, confidence score, and generated payload in real-time.
- **Live Email Dispatch:** Direct integration with SendGrid to immediately dispatch the AI-generated recovery emails to the customer's inbox.
- **KPI Tracking:** Real-time analytics tracking Revenue Recovered, Margin Protected, and Strategy Success Rates.

## 4. Target Audience
- Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) E-commerce Brands
- Shopify Merchants
- High-ticket online retailers who want to protect margins while maximizing recovery rates.

## 5. Value Proposition
"Stop guessing why they left, and stop giving away your margins. RecoverAI intelligently negotiates with your abandoned checkouts using targeted psychology, securing the sale while protecting your profits."
